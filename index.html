01 / THE MECHANISM

Not a black box.
A bounded decision system.

Jev cannot invent a destination, rewrite the rules, or move funds to an arbitrary wallet. The agent selects from holder-first policies published by the vault. The contract verifies the choice, records the epoch, and makes the resulting holder allocation claimable.
